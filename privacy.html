Privacy policy

Effective 29 October 2026

This policy explains what data the Purnakosh app (Android app and web app, together "Purnakosh") uses, where it is kept, and the choices you have. Purnakosh is made by Sujan Shrestha, an individual developer in Nepal ("we", "us").

The short version

  • Your financial data is stored on your device and in a single file Purnakosh creates in your own Google Drive.
  • We don't run a server that stores your financial data, and we never see it. The only things that reach a server we run are listed under "Information sent to other services" below.
  • No ads, no cookies and no analytics service. The app counts anonymous usage with a random ID (no personal or financial data), and you can switch that off in Settings.
  • We never sell your data, and we don't share it with anyone other than the services listed below.

Data you enter

You may enter or import: share holdings and transactions (including Meroshare CSV files you choose), money entries (amounts, categories, accounts, notes, dates), salary and tax figures, vehicle details (registration number, tax and insurance dates, bluebook photos you scan), and the names you give your portfolios (for example, family members). This data is used only to show you your figures in the app.

Where your data is kept

  • On your device, in the app's private storage (on Android) or the browser's storage (on the web, see "Browser storage" below).
  • In your own Google Drive, when you sign in with Google: Purnakosh creates one spreadsheet file, called "Purnakosh", and keeps it in step with your device so your other devices can use the same data. The file belongs to you and stays in your Drive; we have no access to it.
  • Your income tax figures stay on your device only.
  • Bluebook scanning reads the text on the device; the photo isn't uploaded anywhere.

Google account data

When you sign in with Google, Purnakosh asks for:

  • Your name and email address (basic profile), to show which account is signed in and to name your first portfolio.
  • Access to files Purnakosh creates in your Google Drive (the drive.file permission). With this permission Purnakosh can only see and change the file it made itself. It can't see, read or change any of your other Drive files.

This Google data is used only to provide Purnakosh's features to you: saving and syncing your own data in your own Drive. It isn't transferred to anyone else, used for advertising, sold, or read by people. Purnakosh's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Information sent to other services

  • Google (sign-in, Drive and Sheets), as described above, under Google's privacy policy.
  • Market prices: to show current share prices, the app downloads public market data from a price feed run by the developer on Google Apps Script. The request includes the stock symbols you follow, so their prices can be returned. Symbols the feed doesn't track yet are added to its list of symbols, without any information about who asked. The request doesn't include your name, account, holdings or amounts.
  • Anonymous usage count: once a day the app sends the same price feed a random ID made on your device, the app version and the platform (Android or web), so we can see roughly how many installs are in use and which versions. It doesn't include your name, email, Google account, holdings, amounts or any other data you entered, and the random ID can't be traced back to you. We keep one row per ID per day in a Google Sheet that only the developer can open. There is no automatic expiry yet: the rows are kept until the developer deletes them. Turn the check-in off any time in Settings → Usage statistics, or choose "Reset my random ID" to start a new, unrelated ID. To have the rows already sent with your ID deleted, copy the ID shown in the same place (it appears once the app has made one) and email it to the address under "Contact"; the developer deletes every row with that ID.
  • App updates (Android): the app checks Expo's update service for new versions, sending the app's version and platform. See Expo's privacy policy.
  • Links you tap (for example, to download a new version or to read a tax rule) open in your browser.

If you connect Purnakosh to your own Google Apps Script Web App instead of signing in (an advanced option), your data is kept in your own Google Sheet, and any services you set up there are under your control. Those services receive data from that Sheet: AI questions send a summary of the portfolio and your question to the AI provider whose key you saved (Google Gemini or Anthropic), Telegram alerts send the alert text to Telegram, and phone notifications save your phone's Expo push token in the Sheet and send the alert text through Expo's push service.

Browser storage (web app)

The web app sets no cookies and loads no third-party scripts, fonts or trackers. It uses your browser's own storage only for the app to work: IndexedDB and local storage for your data and settings, a short-lived Google access token in local storage after you sign in (up to an hour), session storage for the sign-in step, and the browser's cache so the app opens offline. This storage is strictly needed to provide the app you asked for, which is why there is no cookie banner. You can remove it with "Erase data on this device" in Settings, or by clearing the site's data in your browser.

The web app and this website are served by Cloudflare and GitHub Pages, and the Android app is downloaded from GitHub Releases. As with any website, they receive your IP address and standard request details when you load a page or download a file, and may keep them in their own logs. See Cloudflare's and GitHub's privacy statements.

Phone features

  • Fingerprint or face unlock, to lock the app. Handled by your phone; Purnakosh only learns whether the check passed.
  • Camera, only when you choose to scan a vehicle bluebook.
  • Notifications, for vehicle tax and insurance reminders you've set, scheduled on the device.
  • Files, only to open a CSV file you choose to import.

Keeping or deleting your data

  • Your data stays until you delete it. You can delete entries, portfolios and vehicles in the app.
  • To remove everything from a device: in Settings choose "Erase data on this device". It removes the portfolio, money entries, vehicles and reminders, plans, tax figures, settings and Google sign-in from that phone or browser and restarts the app. It does not touch your Drive file or a Web App sheet, and changes that were never saved to either are lost. (Signing out and choosing "Remove from this device" removes only the portfolio.) Uninstalling the Android app, or clearing the site's data in your browser, also removes it.
  • To delete the copy in your Drive: delete the "Purnakosh" file in Google Drive.
  • To stop Purnakosh's access to your Google account: remove it at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Security

Data on your device is kept in the app's private storage and can be locked behind your phone's fingerprint or PIN. Data travels to Google over encrypted connections (HTTPS). No method of storage or transfer is completely secure, but we keep the app's access to the minimum it needs.

Children

Purnakosh is meant for adults managing their own or their household's finances. It isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date, and important changes will be shown in the app's "What's new".
