Questions

How is income tax calculated on salary in Nepal?

Your taxable income is salary and allowances minus allowed deductions such as SSF, CIT, insurance and donations. The tax is then worked out slab by slab. Purnakosh does this for you and shows each step.

Does it include SSF and CIT?

Yes. Choose SSF, PF or CIT and the deductions are applied to your taxable income.

Can it estimate monthly TDS?

Yes. It shows the TDS your employer is likely to deduct each month.
