Questions

How is capital gains tax on shares calculated in Nepal?

It is charged on your profit, at one rate for shares held up to a year and a lower rate for shares held longer. Purnakosh applies the right rate to each lot you bought and shows the tax before you sell. Check the current rates with the Inland Revenue Department.

What fees are charged when selling shares in NEPSE?

Broker commission, the SEBON fee and the DP charge, plus capital gains tax on any profit. The sell calculator includes all of them.
